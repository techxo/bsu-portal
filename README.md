BSU International Student Portal.
International student web portal services at a fully functional portal developed at Bowie State University. It unites housing search, peer assistance, AI-driven directions, and campus resources into a single location.
Live site: techxo.github.io/bsu-portal

What It Does
Ask Maya - an AI assistant that is trained on knowledge about BSU. Inquire about the F-1 visa regulations, CPT and OPT permission, student accommodation, banking, scholarships, career guidance, or anything related to the life in the Bowie State campus. She provides actual, specific answers not general ones.
Housing Search — view 9 trusted listings with real pictures, prices, bedroom, and bathroom details, occupancy status, and contact number of the agent. By Type, Price, bedrooms, and Availability. Save favorites. Upload your own listing.
Mentor Network - network with 8 professional mentors in STEM, Business, Career Services, Finance, Immigration Law, Wellness, and Research. Every mentor will have a compatibility score, live availability status, and a thread of direct messages.
Direct Messaging- chat directly with any mentor you are connected to. Discussions are left and continuous during your session. The role-specific guidance is responded by mentors.
Resource library- selected handouts on immigration, banking, campus life, transportation, health insurance, and scholarships. Categorized and searchable.
FAQ Center 8 most popular questions that are asked by international students, and they are answered in detail. Filterable by subject and searchable.
